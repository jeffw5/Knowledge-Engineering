        Assumptions behind the plan

        • Scope is only the eight component sets of the Sales Advisor design. Quoting, CRM, payroll and enrollment systems are consumed as they are, through the virtual graph.
        • Durations are in weeks for one dedicated squad per component set working in parallel; ontology stewards, rule owners and SMEs join for the Keel, the SSOT rules and the golden answers.
        • Buffers total : 1 week for the Keel, 2 for the content graph (covering the year-end holidays), 2 for the advisor launch and a 1-week project buffer before G3.
        • Value at stake uses the 12 core job steps, their baseline gaps from the advisor’s value-gap monitor (synthetic design values) and the tier-H counts from the decision-question baseline. The other desired outcomes enter when their baselines are measured; jobs J2 and J3 move to Q2 2027.
        • Work starts on Monday 19 October 2026 and must finish by 31 March 2027. Dates are planning dates, not commitments.
