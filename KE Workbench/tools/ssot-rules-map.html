SSOT Rules & Standards Map

The logic, rules and constraints the SSOT must hold to build the Sales Advisor: governance on demand, policy conformance per control, decisions, and orchestration through events, plus 51 executable constraints, 50 analytics calculations, the agents and microservices that consume each, and the calcs published downstream. Existing register rules are reused where they fit; the gaps are filled by 94 proposed rules in the register’s own 14-field format. Every rule is mapped to the standards and policies it implements, including the ISO/IEC 27000 family.

How the four functions work together at run time

A persona question or agent action calls the SSOT as a service, controls produce evidence on every step, decisions are gated and recorded, and events carry the result onward and trigger the next rule.

Proposed rules by domain

What changes in the SSOT

  • Rules become a service. GOD rules make every Released rule callable at run time with its version, effective date, explanation and any waiver.
  • Every obligation gets a control. CTL rules map obligations to ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A, SOC 2 and NIST 800-53 controls with automated tests and evidence.
  • Decisions are governed objects. DEC rules set decision rights, option completeness, human confirmation, neutrality and immutable records.
  • Triggers become events. EVT rules turn each register rule’s Trigger_Condition into an event pattern and define the envelope, ordering, retries and privacy of events.
  • Agents are governed like controls. AIG rules register all 38 agents and give each an MTBH circuit breaker, probes and an owner.
  • Sales conduct is covered. SAL rules handle licensed producers, savings claims, outreach consent, PEO licence disclosure and accessibility.

Calcs published for downstream analytics

Published to the semantic layer under a stable metric name. Each needs a Released Metric Definition Card (MET-014) before publication, carries pinned SSOT rule versions, and applies small-cut suppression (k ≥ 5) where people could be identified.
