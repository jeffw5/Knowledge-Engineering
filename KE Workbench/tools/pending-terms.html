Pending terms

A label or concept proposal that cannot be resolved to an existing concept in its scope does not create a concept. It waits here, in DEV and STG only, until the data steward chooses exactly one disposition. Each decision passes the AI circuit breaker gateway like every other decision. A release that still contains a pending term fails the label resolution check.
