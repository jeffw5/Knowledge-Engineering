# Business Operations

The operational area that runs the organization's own business (D26): Finance, Accounting, HR, Billing & AR, Compliance, Legal and Security.

| Folder | Asset names | IRIs |
|---|---|---|
| `business-operations/` | Business Operations Vocabulary, Taxonomy, Ontology, Shapes (0.1.0): the area's shared assets and a taxonomy of its domains | `…/<kind>/business-operations/` |
| `legal/` | Domain Legal (0.2.0), moved from Commercial Operations; commercial and regulatory law, agreements and contracts | `…/<kind>/domain-legal/` |

The other domains are built as the area is modeled. Their internal domain services are built on the APQC Process Classification Framework, mainly categories 7.0 (human capital), 9.0 (finance) and 11.0 (risk and compliance), which still need loading from a licensed copy.
