# HR Service Operations

The operational area that delivers the external services of the HR Services & PEO Enterprise Business Model to clients and their worksite employees (D25, D26). Its assets are titled "Human Resources Service Operations …" (HR is spelled out under Part III, and "HR Service Operations" is kept as the alternative label).

| Folder | Asset names | IRIs |
|---|---|---|
| `hr-service-operations/` | Human Resources Service Operations Vocabulary, Taxonomy, Ontology, Shapes (0.1.0): the area's shared assets and a taxonomy of its eight domains | `…/<kind>/hr-service-operations/` |

The eight L1 domains are the business model's domains:
- Workforce Administration & Payroll
- Employee Benefits & Retirement Sponsorship
- Government Compliance & Employer Risk Management
- Talent Management & Organizational Development
- Client Relationship & Business Development
- HR Technology Platform & Employee Experience
- Workforce Development, Coaching & Performance Measurement
- Organizational Values, Culture & Ethics Stewardship

Their L2 domains are the bounded contexts, and their L3 domains are the domain services. They are built next, by ingesting the business model; its services then become the external portfolio of the enterprise service catalog.
