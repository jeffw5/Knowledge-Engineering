Circuit breaker & drift monitor

Rejection rate is tracked per agent over its last 10 proposals. Crossing 40% trips that agent's breaker only — no other agent is paused. Value-drift is the same rolling rejection rate expressed as a coefficient. Click a card for full agent detail; use "Run only this agent" to test one domain in isolation.

Drag & drop agentic workflow orchestration

How this works (click to expand)

Palette →Canvas: drag a pipeline-stage tile or a domain-agent tile from the left onto the canvas. Click one tile, then another, to draw a connecting line between them (this is purely visual — it illustrates the flow, it doesn't change what runs).

What actually controls the run: only the agent tiles you place matter functionally — they decide which domain cartridges participate. The five pipeline-stage tiles (Router, Validation, Reconciliation, Commit, Monitor) are optional on the canvas; they always execute internally regardless, dragging them on just animates that step during a run.

Fastest way to see it work: click "Load a 3-agent starter pipeline" below for an instant, ready-to-run example, or just drag one agent tile onto the canvas and click Run.
