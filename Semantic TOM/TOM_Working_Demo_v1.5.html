How the operating model works

This demo walks through the TOM's operating model, operations and measurement on one simulated PEO operation. Every answer Compass gives depends on five supply-chain links; telemetry becomes calibrated measures, which roll up into service levels, unit costs, customer outcomes and policy adherence. The same causal model that explains a past spike also simulates a proposed change. Click any box to open its page, or start at the Operations command center, which drills from the big picture down to lineage, trace and audit records.

INSTRUMENT · METROLOGY

Metrology: can we trust the measures?

A measure is only useful if it is defined once, measured the same way every time, and known to be accurate. Each measure has a registry entry in the SKL with its method, unit, uncertainty and calibration status. Measurement-system checks run continuously.

Measure registry

Every gating measure has an owner, a method, an uncertainty and a calibration date. Drifting or overdue measures cannot gate a promotion.

Repeatability: the same evaluation set, run 30 times

Spread shows measurement noise, not service change. A change smaller than the spread is not a real change.

Telemetry quality by link

Completeness of spans received and median lag before a signal is usable.

Human graders: inter-rater agreement

Groundedness and Expert Agreement depend on human judgement. Cohen's kappa by grader pair; below 0.6 triggers grader recalibration.

ANALYZE · CAUSAL ANALYSIS

Causal analysis: why did it move?

The SKL links each measure to the measures and levers that drive it. Pick an outcome and a day; the analysis compares that day with the baseline (days 1–55) and splits the change across its root causes using Shapley attribution over the causal model.

Outcome Day

Bridge from the baseline value to the selected day. Each bar is one root cause's share of the change.

Causal graph

Root causes (left) drive link measures, which drive service and outcome measures. Edges on the path to the selected outcome are highlighted; width shows strength of the effect on this day.
