Survey modules and sample plan

Each respondent qualifies into exactly one persona module and rates every outcome of the jobs that persona executes. Modules over 80 outcomes are split in two and assigned at random. Intervals are 95% half-widths on an opportunity score at the most uncertain point.

Method notes

Jobs are the client's, not the provider's. The ontology describes what the PEO delivers. Each job states what a client persona is trying to get done, without naming a solution, and links back to the services that help.

Outcome format. Direction + metric + object of control + clarifier. Metric type is read from the statement: time (speed), likelihood (stability), cost or liability, effort, or count (output).

Themes. The ontology's nine Core Service Lines, assigned from each outcome's primary service, plus PEO Relationship and HR Technology & Data for services the service-line layer does not cover.

Before fielding. Validate the statements in 15–20 interviews (two to three per persona), then pilot each module with 20–30 respondents.
