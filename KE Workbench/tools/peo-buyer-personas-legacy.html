Two instances of the same taxonomy-core-persona/ ontology used for the Dana Whitfield commuter instance, populated for the two buyer personas the PEO Sales Advisor serves. Fields marked from advisor model come straight from the Sales Advisor design: its job maps, decision scenarios, value-gap measurements, SSOT extraction rules (EXT-001–016) and governance controls (GOV-007, GOV-011, GOV-014). Those figures are themselves synthetic design values, not Insperity data. Fields marked illustrative (names, emails, phone numbers, cities, exact dates) are placeholders. proposed extension marks the one class the ontology lacks: it has PersonaType subclasses only for engineering roles, so each buyer persona is asserted as a new isp-sal subclass of PersonaType, pending steward review. Unlike the commuter case, almost every class is instantiated here, because the advisor runs the personalization, feedback and selection loops that the smart-city pilots never had. The Job study tab maps each persona to its jobs, job steps and desired outcomes (written as Minimize/Maximize statements with a metric, target and metric card). Baselines are filled only where the advisor’s value-gap monitor already measures them; importance and satisfaction columns are left empty for the outcome survey.
