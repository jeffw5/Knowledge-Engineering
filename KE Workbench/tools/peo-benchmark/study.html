Method and data notes

Scoring. Importance and satisfaction are the share of respondents rating an outcome 4 or 5 on a 5-point scale, divided by 10. Opportunity = I + max(I − S, 0). Tiers: above 15 extreme, 12–15 high, 10–12 solid, below 10 limited. An outcome is over-served when satisfaction exceeds importance. These are the same rules as the Zephyr study.

Segments. PEO clients currently co-employ through a PEO. Non-PEO combines in-house HR and payroll/HRO or ASO providers. Total is the respondent-weighted combination.

Sample. Each outcome is rated by the persona modules that execute its job, so n differs by outcome. The 95% intervals use each outcome's own n and assume independent importance and satisfaction estimates.

Loading real results. Upload a CSV with the columns outcome_id, segment, n, importance, satisfaction. Segment is PEO or NONPEO; an optional TOTAL row overrides the weighted total.
