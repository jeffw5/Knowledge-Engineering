APQC Process Classification Framework 7.4

The process framework the Commercial Operations domains are built on: Sales, Sales.Partner & Channel, Marketing and Pricing on category 3.0, Customer Care on category 6.0. Each domain instantiates the activities (L4) it owns; their tasks (L5) are its steps (design decision D28).
