What the map says about Insperity

Read-out

Where the baseline is strong

  • Enterprise HCM path inside the PEO. HRScale puts Workday HCM, payroll, absence and time under Insperity's co-employment for 150–5,000-employee firms. ADP (Workforce Now) and Rippling are the only peers with a comparable one-platform scale-up story.
  • Breadth of human-delivered services in the bundle. Performance management, training and development, organizational planning, recruiting and outplacement support appear as Insperity services; most peers deliver these as software modules or not at all.
  • Benefits anchor. A single UnitedHealthcare master plan running since 2002 and extended through 2028.

Where competitors set the pace

  • Vertical packaging. TriNet sells industry-specific service teams (tech, life sciences, nonprofit, financial and professional services, hospitality, retail). Insperity has no packaged vertical offer in public material.
  • Price transparency. Justworks publishes per-employee rates and moved PEO Plus from $109 to $124 in June 2026. Every other provider, Insperity included, is quote-only.
  • Global and EOR. Justworks, Rippling, TriNet and VensureHR offer international hiring. Nothing comparable appears for Insperity.
  • Lighter-weight entry tiers. TriNet HR Plus, Paychex HR Outsourcing and VensureHR offer ASO without co-employment; Rippling lets clients switch the PEO off and keep the software.
  • AI narrative. Paychex now leads its corporate positioning with the WISE agentic AI engine.

Validate before using

  • Red Verify cells in the Insperity column are services the public record does not confirm. Check them against the internal product catalog first; that turns the baseline into ground truth.
  • Ratings come from vendor pages, filings and third-party reviews. Vendor comparison pages (for example ADP's claims about competitors' learning systems) are marked as vendor claims.
  • Service IDs are stable so the matrix can seed a SKOS concept scheme; use Copy SKOS to start one.
