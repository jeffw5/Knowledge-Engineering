How to edit

Drop on a term → becomes its narrower term
Drop on the top edge of a term → becomes its broader term
Hold Alt/⌥ while dropping to add a second broader term instead of moving.
A vocabulary term dropped here becomes a taxonomy concept named after its label (UpperCamelCase), with its label and definition, linked back with core:hasPrimaryTerm and dcterms:subject (§3.11). A concept already linked to that term, or with the same label, is reused.
