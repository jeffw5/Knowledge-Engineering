Persona instances are read live from GraphDB (jtbd:client-persona in any graph of the repository set in the workbench) through proxy.py. When GraphDB is not reachable the page shows the bundled Customer 360 schema and PEO Outcome Benchmark instances from data/c360/, plus any interview or demand drafts saved in this browser. Property names and definitions come from the ontology graphs themselves, so a property added in the Model Editor appears here without code changes. Scores in the Job study tab come from the Job Study (example data until a survey wave is loaded there); review status comes from the last PEO Outcome Review export imported in the Demand Gateway. The earlier hand-authored Sales Advisor instances (Marisol Reyes, Jordan Ellis) are kept in the legacy persona page.
